Key ISO 14971 Regulatory Definitions

Continuous Risk Management Lifecycle

ISO 14971:2019 describes an iterative risk management process extending from design and development through post-production activities.

Status: Iterative Lifecycle Active
STEP 01 (Clause 4.4)

Risk Management Planning

Define scope, policy, criteria and the risk management plan.

STEP 02 (Clause 5)

Risk Analysis

Identify hazards and hazardous situations; estimate risk.

STEP 03 (Clause 6)

Risk Evaluation

Compare estimated risks against defined acceptability criteria.

STEP 04 (Clause 7)

Risk Control

Select, implement and verify appropriate risk control measures.

STEP 05 (Clause 8)

Residual Risk & Benefit Review

Evaluate residual risks and overall residual risk acceptability.

STEP 06 (Clauses 9 & 10)

RMF Report & Post-Market

Review production and post-production information and feed it back into risk management.
